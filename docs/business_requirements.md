# Business Requirements

## Business Scenario

This project models the end-to-end data flow of a manufacturing business that provides laser cutting, fabrication, external processing, delivery, and invoicing services.

## Main Business Processes

1. Customer enquiry is received.
2. A quotation is prepared and sent.
3. The customer submits a purchase order.
4. Materials are checked or purchased.
5. The job is programmed and scheduled.
6. Production is completed using assigned machines.
7. Fabrication or external processing may be required.
8. The job is packed and delivered.
9. An invoice is issued.

## Key Business Questions

- Which jobs are approaching their due date?
- Which machines have available capacity?
- Which materials must be purchased?
- How long does each production stage take?
- Which jobs are delayed?
- Which customers generate the most revenue?
- What is the lead time from purchase order to delivery?
- How much work is assigned to each machine?

## Data Sources

The initial project will use synthetic CSV files representing:

- Customers
- Quotations
- Purchase orders
- Jobs
- Materials
- Machine schedules
- Production activities
- Deliveries
- Invoices

## Data Privacy

All data used in this project is synthetic. No real customer, employee, supplier, pricing, or company-confidential information is included.
